One AI reviewing your plan will be polite. Two AIs arguing about it will be useful. Use two different models (for example Claude and ChatGPT) — not two chats with the same one.


---
Step 1.  Give the same plan to both. Ask each the same three questions: What is our riskiest unstated assumption? Why might this fail by week 6? What is missing from our evaluation?
Step 2.  Cross the reviews: give each model the other’s critique. Ask it to attack or defend it.
Step 3.  Write the short log: where they agreed (probably a real problem), where they disagreed (you decide, and say why), and the one change you made to the plan because of it.
